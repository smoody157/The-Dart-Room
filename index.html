HOW TO PLAY 01

Choose 301, 501, or 701. Players take turns throwing up to three darts. Each turn subtracts the scored points from that player's total.

Use the selected in/out mode. In straight-in mode, scoring starts immediately. In double-in mode, the player must hit a double before points count. In straight-out mode, any valid finish can win. In double-out mode, the final dart must be a double.

A player wins by reaching exactly zero. If the turn goes below zero, leaves one when double-out is required, or violates the out rule, the turn is a bust and the previous score is restored.

HOW TO PLAY KILLER

Each player is assigned a target number. Players throw at their own number to become active, then attack opponents by hitting the opponents' assigned numbers.

Hits remove lives from the target player. Singles, doubles, and triples can count according to the active game settings. A player is eliminated when their lives reach zero.

The winner is the last player still alive.

HOW TO PLAY CRICKET

Cricket uses 20, 19, 18, 17, 16, 15, and Bull. Each number closes after three marks. Singles count one mark, doubles count two marks, and triples count three marks.

Bull has single and double only.

Once a player closes a number, extra marks on that number can score points until the opponent also closes it.

The winner is the player who closes all cricket numbers and has the highest score, or who closes everything while tied or ahead.
